Questions, answered.

Is CycleALIGN a regular period tracker?

It tracks your period, flow, mood and symptoms like any good tracker. It also turns your cycle phase into practical guidance for your calendar, your decisions and your spending.

Who is it for?

Women founders, leaders and professionals who want to work with their biology, not push through it.

How does the phase-based calendar work?

You connect your calendar, and the app suggests dates for pitches, reviews and deep work that fit your current phase. It can also suggest a better time to move a meeting.

Is CycleALIGN free?

CycleALIGN is free to download on the App Store and Google Play.

What is Partner Mode?

Partner Mode helps your partner understand your cycle, so you can have better conversations and build stronger bonds.

Which phones does it work on?

CycleALIGN is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

Do I need to know my exact cycle length?

No. Start with your best estimate. Cycles vary, and your predictions improve as you log.

Can I use it for birth control or medical decisions?

No. CycleALIGN offers planning guidance, not medical advice. Please talk to your doctor about health concerns.
