CycleALIGN vs Nua: Which Period App Is Right for Indian Working Women?

📅 July 2026 ⏱ 9 min read ✍ Vinita Thakur

An honest, no-marketing-jargon comparison of two Indian-built apps that approach the same 28-day cycle from opposite directions — one is a full women's wellness ecosystem, the other is a focused leadership productivity tool.

The short answer, upfront

Both apps are built for Indian women. Both track your period. That is where the similarity ends.

Nua is one of India's most respected D2C women's wellness brands, best known for its sanitary pads, PCOS supplements, and skincare products. Nua recently launched its own period tracker app that bundles cycle tracking with a shop for their full product range and subscription management. If you already trust Nua's products and want an all-in-one wellness experience — track your period, order your pads, manage your PCOS routine, all in one place — Nua is a natural choice.

CycleALIGN is a cycle-aware leadership app built for women worldwide — the first of its kind out of India, now serving working women globally. It tracks your cycle too, but the tracking is the input, not the product. What CycleALIGN does with that data is different: it scores your work tasks against your hormonal phase, forecasts your energy for the week ahead, and tells you the best day to pitch, negotiate, or present. If your goal is professional performance, CycleALIGN is built specifically for that.

These are not competing products. They are complementary tools serving very different moments in a woman's life. The question is which one is built for what you actually want to do.

What each app is genuinely great at

Where Nua wins

  • Full women's wellness ecosystem — Nua bundles period tracking with sanitary pads, PCOS supplements, cramp relief patches, and skincare — everything a menstruating woman needs in one Indian app.
  • Trusted Indian brand — Nua has built genuine trust with Indian women over years through its content, product quality, and community. That trust extends to the app.
  • Integrated shopping and subscriptions — Manage your Nua product subscriptions, get delivery reminders, and reorder easily without leaving the app.
  • Personalised product recommendations — The tracker suggests Nua products based on where you are in your cycle and what you actually need.
  • Free to use — The tracker itself is free; Nua monetises through its D2C product sales.
  • Educational content library — Nua has invested years in producing accessible, culturally relevant content on women's health for Indian audiences.

Where CycleALIGN wins

  • Task Sync — phase-aware scheduling — Add a task and a date, CycleALIGN scores it against your phase. Green means ideal timing, red means reschedule. No other Indian period app does this.
  • Daily morning insight — One personalised two-line brief every morning based on where you are in your cycle today. Focused on how to lead your day, not on what product to buy.
  • Weekly phase forecast — Every Monday, your week ahead mapped to your hormonal phases so you can plan meetings, launches, and rest days deliberately.
  • Role-specific personalisation — The app speaks differently to a founder, a corporate professional, a homemaker, and a student. Your insights match your actual life.
  • Grounded in endocrinology and leadership research — CycleALIGN is built on peer-reviewed science on hormonal fluctuation, cognition, and decision-making — applied to how women actually work.
  • Privacy-first task input — You never have to type what the task actually is. Category only. Your sensitive plans stay yours.
  • Not a shopping app — CycleALIGN is a focused productivity tool, not a storefront. Every screen is designed around your work life, not upsells.
